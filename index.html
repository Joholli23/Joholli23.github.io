About Me

My name is Josh and I grew up in central Florida. After high school, I spent a couple of years working before joining the Air Force. The Air Force gave me many different opportunities and sent me to many different locations. A few of those locations included Hawaii, Alaska, South Korea, and Kuwait. Halfway through my career I met my wife and we had a couple of kids. My daughter was born in 2014 and my son was born 13 months after her in 2015. I retired from the Air Force in 2023 and we moved from Alaska to North Carolina to be closer to family. I am currently working as a contractor on Fort Bragg and decided to broaden my skill set with a degree in Computer Science. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my family, playing video games, and playing golf.
